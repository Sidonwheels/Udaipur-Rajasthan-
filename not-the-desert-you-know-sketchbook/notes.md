# Not the Desert You Know: sketchbook notes

Rough overview (20 to 22 Aug 2026, monsoon season)

- **20 Aug, Udaipur:** old-city rooftop in the morning, Bahubali Hills over Badi Lake at midday, a coffee shop stop, old-city lanes, dinner and the lakeshore at night (01 to 14).
- **21 Aug, Udaipur and around:** green Aravalli hills in the morning, sweets, a long City Palace visit in the afternoon, sunset over the lake, a boat ride and a rooftop dinner (15 to 44).
- **22 Aug, Banswara:** Chacha Kota on the Mahi backwaters, a grassy hilltop and then boating among the islands (45 to 50).

The point of the trip: Rajasthan is not only desert. Lakes, green hills and backwaters all show up here.
